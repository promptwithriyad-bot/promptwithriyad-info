Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Prompt With Riyad Manager is a private tool used by one person, the owner of the promptwithriyad channels, to publish their own videos and manage their own accounts. It has no other users.

What the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's own channel, to read, reply to and delete comments on those videos, and to read basic channel and video statistics.
  • Facebook and Instagram: permission to publish videos to the owner's own Page and Instagram account, to read, reply to and delete comments on those posts, and to read their insights and follower counts.

What the app stores

Account access tokens, the videos and captions it publishes, and the public comments left on the owner's posts together with the replies it sent. This is kept on a private server controlled by the owner.

How the information is used

Only to publish the owner's content, reply to comments on it, and show the owner their own statistics. The information is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train any model.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account permissions page and from the Facebook and Instagram settings under connected apps. Removing access stops the app. Stored data can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Contact

Send a message to @promptwithriyad on Instagram.
